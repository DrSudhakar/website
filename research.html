Research

How does the brain construct emotional experience — and how can we build machines that recognise and respond to it responsibly?

Themes

Spatiotemporal dynamics of emotion

EEG microstates, functional/effective connectivity and multifractal analyses of emotional events, built on a naturalistic Indian EEG dataset (DENS) and the Emotional Event Marker Paradigm.

Affective computing

Emotion recognition from EEG, cardiac and facial signals; VAD-space and fuzzy representations; deep sequential and transformer models.

RL for language models

Reinforcement learning from verifiable rewards, curricula for post-training, and the robustness of RL-trained empathetic agents.

Contemplative science

How practices such as Tibetan monastic debate change emotion regulation, absorption and brain activity.
