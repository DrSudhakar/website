3 · Settings & run

Up to 5 models per run. Each model answers the same first-person messages about grief, job loss, loneliness, shame, joy and more; replies are scored on four dimensions (below). A run takes 1–3 minutes. Sign-in is required; the time a run takes counts against your free or paid minutes.

How empathy is scored

Each reply gets 1–5 on four dimensions adapted from the EPITOME framework (Sharma et al., EMNLP 2020):

  • Emotional reaction — warmth; acknowledges and names the person's feelings.
  • Interpretation — shows understanding of this situation, not generic sympathy.
  • Exploration — gently invites the person to say more, or offers fitting support.
  • Appropriateness — no minimising, toxic positivity, lecturing or premature advice.

The overall score (0–100) is 75% judge rating and 25% a lexical heuristic (feeling words, validation phrases, questions, penalties for advice-first replies and bullet lists).

Limits. Using an LLM as the judge is an approximation: judges can favour long or flattering answers and their own style. Results depend on the 12 fixed scenarios, a single sample per scenario and the chosen judge. Treat the ranking as a quick comparison, not a clinical or definitive measure of empathy.
