Cardiac activity from your webcam

Each heartbeat changes how much light your skin absorbs. A webcam can pick up that tiny colour pulse (remote photoplethysmography, rPPG) and turn it into heart rate and heart-rate variability.

🔒 Private by design. Video is processed on your device only; nothing is recorded or uploaded. Your browser will ask for camera permission.
⚕️ This is a research / teaching demo, not a medical device. HRV from a 30 fps webcam is only indicative.

Tips: face the camera in steady, even light (daylight is best), keep still and don't talk. Remove strong backlight.

How it works

  1. Find the face with MediaPipe Face Landmarker and sample three skin regions: forehead and both cheeks.
  2. Average the red, green, blue values in those regions for every frame.
  3. Separate pulse from motion/lighting using the POS algorithm (Wang et al., 2017), then band-pass 0.7–3.5 Hz (42–210 bpm).
  4. Heart rate = strongest spectral peak; HRV from the intervals between detected beats (RMSSD, SDNN).

My M.Tech. work used a different video cue — head ballistocardiography — see “Heart rate measurement using video in different user states”.
