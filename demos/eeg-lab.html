EEG microstate & affect lab

Upload EEG recordings from one or more tasks. The service preprocesses them automatically, (1) builds microstates and compares them between tasks, and (2) runs affective computing analyses with open-source models you choose.

1 · Data

One file per task/condition (e.g. “calm film”, “fear film”). Accepted: .edf .bdf .set .fif .csv .npy, up to 30 MB in total. For CSV/NPY give the sampling rate. Channel names should follow the 10-20 system (Fz, Cz, O1 …) for scalp maps. Data are processed in memory and not stored.

Privacy. EEG can be personal data. Only upload recordings you have the right and consent to share (ideally anonymised).

2 · Preprocessing & microstates

Automatic steps: crop (≤ 5 min/task) → bad-channel detection & interpolation → notch → band-pass → resample to 250 Hz → average reference → rejection of 2-s epochs with peak-to-peak > 150 µV. Microstates: group templates by polarity-invariant modified K-means on GFP peaks pooled across tasks, back-fitting to every task, then coverage / duration / occurrence / transitions per task and statistics between tasks.
