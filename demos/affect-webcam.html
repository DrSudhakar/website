Affective expression profile

Your webcam feed is turned into 52 facial-muscle “blendshape” signals, which are combined into basic-emotion scores and a valence–arousal trace over time.

🔒 Private by design. Everything runs on your device; no video or scores are uploaded.
What this does and does not measure. It estimates facial movement, which is only loosely related to what someone feels (context, culture and individual differences matter). Use it for learning and exploration, not for judging people.

How it works

MediaPipe Face Landmarker outputs blendshape coefficients that correspond closely to FACS action units (e.g. mouthSmile ≈ AU12, browDown ≈ AU4). Each basic emotion is scored from the action units that EMFACS associates with it (the weights are in assets/js/affect.js and are easy to change), then valence and arousal are the score-weighted positions of those emotions on the circumplex.
